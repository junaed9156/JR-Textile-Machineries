    FAQ

    Buying Textile Machinery — Questions Answered

    Air jet looms, water jet looms and spare parts for factories in Bangladesh, India and Pakistan.

    Where can I buy textile weaving machines in Bangladesh?

    JR Textile Machineries sells air jet looms, water jet looms, repair looms and loom spare parts sourced directly from Chinese manufacturers. We have offices in Narsingdi and Rupganj (Narayanganj) in Bangladesh and in Yangzhou, China, and deliver machines to your factory floor.

    Do you supply textile machinery to India and Pakistan?

    Yes. Besides Bangladesh, we source and ship looms and spare parts for textile factories in India and Pakistan. Send us your requirement on WhatsApp and we will quote with delivery to your country.

    Do I need an import license to buy a loom from China?

    No. Whether or not you have an import license, we handle customs clearance, port documentation and duties for you, from China to your factory.

    Which loom brands and spare parts do you supply?

    We source looms and genuine spare parts for leading weaving machine brands including Toyota, Tsudakoma, Picanol, Dornier, Itema, Somet, Sulzer, Smit and Stäubli.

    How much does an air jet loom or water jet loom cost?

    Prices depend on the model, width, configuration and quantity. Tell us the machine type and quantity and we send a full quotation with specifications, lead time and delivery cost within 24 hours.

    Do you provide installation, training and after-sales support?

    Yes. Our technicians support machine installation, operator training and ongoing maintenance after every sale.
